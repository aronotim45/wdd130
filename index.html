Uganda

Uganda is a beautiful country in East Africa, known as the Pearl of Africa. It is home to diverse cultures, welcoming communities, green landscapes, lakes, and rich wildlife. Its capital city is Kampala, and English and Swahili are its official languages. Uganda is also known for the source of the River Nile and the mountain gorillas of Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.
